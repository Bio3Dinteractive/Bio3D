Human anatomy essentials

Organisation

The human body is organised into cells, tissues, organs and organ systems. Different systems work together to maintain homeostasis and support life.

Skeletal system

The adult human skeleton normally contains 206 bones. Bones provide support, protect organs, allow movement with muscles and store minerals.

Muscular system

Skeletal muscles attach to bones and produce movement. Smooth and cardiac muscle perform involuntary functions.

Circulatory system

The heart pumps blood through pulmonary and systemic circuits.

Respiratory system

Air travels through the respiratory tract to the lungs. Gas exchange occurs across the alveoli.

Digestive system

The digestive tract breaks food into smaller molecules that can be absorbed.

Nervous system

The brain, spinal cord and peripheral nerves coordinate sensation, movement and responses.

Female reproductive system

The ovaries, uterine tubes, uterus, cervix and vagina form the major reproductive structures.

Male reproductive system

The testes, epididymides, ductus deferens and accessory glands form major reproductive structures.
